How to Use & FAQ

How to Use

  1. Mount your phone on a tripod (or similar) so it can see your full swing
  2. Choose a delay of 10 or 15 seconds
  3. Tap "Check Swing!" to start, then take your swing as normal
  4. After the delay, your swing plays back automatically on screen so you can check your form right away

The front (selfie) camera is also supported, so you can check your swing from a face-on angle too.

Why a Delay Camera Helps Your Golf Swing

Watching your own swing is one of the fastest ways to improve your form, but normal video recording means stopping to rewind a clip, or asking someone else to film you.

This tool uses a "timeshift" playback: your camera feed is delayed by a few seconds and replayed automatically on the same screen. Take your swing, wait a moment, and watch it happen on the same screen — no rewinding, no extra device, no interruption to your practice rhythm.

Everything happens locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server or saved anywhere, so you can use it freely at the range or in your backyard without worrying about your footage.

FAQ

Is my video saved anywhere?

No. Video is processed temporarily on your device only — nothing is uploaded or stored, and it's cleared when you close the page.

Do I need to install an app?

No installation needed. It runs entirely in your browser — just open this page and you're ready to go.

What devices and browsers are supported?

Any smartphone or tablet with a camera, using a modern browser such as Safari or Chrome.

Is it free?

Yes, this tool is completely free to use.
