Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Golf Swing Timeshift Camera ("the Site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Site and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The Site processes camera video entirely in your browser. Recorded video and audio are never uploaded to or stored on any server; all processing happens locally on your device.

The following information may still be collected automatically when you visit the Site:

  • Access date/time, referring URL, and pages viewed
  • Browser and device type
  • Approximate country of access (used to automatically select the display language)

2. Use of Cookies

The Site may use cookies for advertising and traffic analysis purposes. Cookies do not identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the Site may not function correctly as a result.

3. Third-Party Advertising

The Site uses third-party advertising services. These providers may use cookies (such as anonymous identifiers, which do not include your name, address, email, or phone number) to serve ads based on your interests.

  • Google AdSense: Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Please refer to each provider's own privacy policy for details on how they use cookies.

4. Analytics

The Site may use analytics tools to understand traffic patterns. These tools use cookies but do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

The Site does not sell, trade, or otherwise share collected information with third parties except as required by law.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time without prior notice. The revised policy will take effect as soon as it is posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on the Site's GitHub repository: 2025yoppi/golf_camera.
